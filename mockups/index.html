Design study

Six ways to draw a hundred thousand bird records

The map currently draws clustered discs with a count inside each one. The number is an artefact of the clustering algorithm rather than a fact about birds — pan the map and it changes — and the discs hide the very thing a distribution map is for. These six treatments all read the same — records: the part of the 265,644-record European export that falls over Britain and Ireland, across 452 species and the 31 days to 24 October.

Density ramp: one hue (amber, OKLCH hue 68°), lightness strictly rising, darkest step receding into the surface. Categorical marks use three validated slots plus a neutral “Other”; all-pairs CVD ΔE 9.4, normal-vision ΔE 20.9 on this surface.
